# Errors found, and how the design surfaced them

All data in the packet is invented. This lists the 18 findings the workpaper raises as cases. Every one was found by code comparing records in the packet, not by hand; each becomes a case in the **Review queue** with both sides of the disagreement, the source record and a next action.

**How to read the table**
- **Source** gives the packet's own ids: `D##` is a document, `ev-####` an extracted value (document, page, field), `qa-##` a questionnaire answer, `call-01` the 26 March call notes, `agency:/…` an IRS or FTB record. The line keys (`SchC.1` and so on) are lines of the v3 return.
- **Click path:** open the app, then follow it. "Review queue > *title*" means the case with that title in the Review queue (search for a few words of it). In the guided flow, **Step 1** says what is wrong in plain words with the estimated effect, and **Step 2 (Check the proof)** shows the source page with the field highlighted and the two sides compared. "Show full details" gives the older three-pane view.
- **Estimated effect** is an estimate: the change multiplied by the packet's per-dollar rates for v3 (`tax_effect_rates`), not a recomputed return. Positive means more tax. The one exception is the HVAC row, which is the tax software's own what-if figure. Federal and California.
- **Re-verified** says what I re-read from the packet in the session that wrote this file. "Source" means the cited records, values and page text. "Federal effect" means I recomputed change × the packet's federal rate by hand and it matched. Anything not stated as re-verified is **not re-verified**.
- **Confirmed by author in the app** is for you to mark. Every row starts at "no".
